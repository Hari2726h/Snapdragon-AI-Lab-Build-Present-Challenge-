## 0:00 - 0:15 | The problem

"A scam arrives when someone is rushed, frightened, or excited. To check it, most tools ask the person to upload the same private message they do not trust. Rakshak Edge changes that."

Show the local protection indicator and open Privacy proof.

## 0:15 - 0:45 | The reveal

Choose **KYC + OTP** and click **Analyze privately**.

"The full analysis happens on this PC. Rakshak finds the credential request, the payment destination, the false urgency, and a look-alike banking domain. The score matters less than the evidence: every warning tells the user what triggered it."

Point to the score, top signals, measured latency, and safest next step.

## 0:45 - 1:05 | A harder scam

Choose **Digital arrest**.

"This example has no malicious link. Rakshak still recognizes authority impersonation, secrecy, call control, and an immediate transfer request. That is where the semantic model adds value beyond link scanners."

## 1:05 - 1:20 | Responsible behavior

Choose **Safe message**.

"The engine understands an awareness message differently from a credential request. A low score never means proven safe, so the interface keeps a verification reminder."

## 1:20 - 1:30 | Snapdragon fit
